# Senior Analytics Engineer (dbt & Snowflake)
**Company**: ClearMetrics
**Department**: Data Analytics
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Transform raw data lakehouse tables into clean, tested, documented dimensional models for business intelligence.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design modular dbt models following Kimball dimensional modeling best practices.
- Implement automated data quality test suites using dbt tests and Great Expectations.
- Optimize Snowflake query performance, clustering keys, and virtual warehouse sizing.
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to define canonical business KPI metrics.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years in analytics engineering or BI development.
- Mastery of SQL, dbt (Core or Cloud), and Snowflake / BigQuery.
- Strong dimensional modeling fundamentals (star schema, facts, dimensions).
- Familiarity with Git version control and CI/CD for analytics.
