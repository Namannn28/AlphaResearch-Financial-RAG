# Disaster Recovery & High-Availability Lead
**Company**: ResilienceGuard
**Department**: Infrastructure Resilience
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Design, test, and automate multi-region failover strategies ensuring zero data loss (RPO=0) during outages.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design multi-region active-active and active-passive architecture patterns on AWS.
- Automate database replication and DNS failover using AWS Route 53 and Global Accelerator.
- Orchestrate quarterly game-day disaster recovery simulation drills.
- Author formal disaster recovery runbooks and executive business continuity reports.

## Required Qualifications
- 6+ years in high-availability systems and disaster recovery.
- Expertise in distributed database replication (Aurora Global Database, CockroachDB).
- Terraform automation mastery.
- Strong incident leadership and documentation skills.
