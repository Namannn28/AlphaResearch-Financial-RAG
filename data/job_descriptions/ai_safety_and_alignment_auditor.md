# AI Safety & Alignment Auditor
**Company**: EthicAI Labs
**Department**: Governance & Trust
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Perform safety red-teaming, bias audits, and regulatory compliance assessments on generative models.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design adversarial prompt injection suites to test model safety guardrails.
- Measure demographic bias and representational parity in generative outputs.
- Author comprehensive AI safety governance reports compliant with EU AI Act standards.
- Collaborate with legal and engineering teams to establish acceptable use policies.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in AI ethics, safety, red-teaming, or model auditing.
- Familiarity with regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF).
- Technical ability to script automated test suites in Python.
- Strong technical writing and cross-functional communication.
