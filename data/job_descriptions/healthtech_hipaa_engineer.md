# HealthTech Data Platform Engineer (HIPAA / FHIR)
**Company**: MediCloud Health
**Department**: Healthcare Informatics
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Build secure, HIPAA-compliant patient health record integration and analytics pipelines using HL7 FHIR standards.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop health data integration pipelines conforming to HL7 FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources).
- Implement strict data-at-rest and data-in-transit encryption, audit logging, and PHI de-identification.
- Integrate with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems (Epic, Cerner) via SMART-on-FHIR APIs.
- Ensure continuous compliance with HIPAA Security Rules and SOC 2 audits.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years in healthcare software engineering.
- Hands-on experience with HL7, FHIR, and EHR integrations.
- Strong familiarity with HIPAA compliance and Protected Health Information (PHI) handling.
- Proficiency in Python, Java, or C#.
