# Computer Vision & Edge ML Engineer
**Company**: Visionary AI
**Department**: Perception Systems
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Train and optimize real-time object detection and segmentation models for edge deployment.

## Key Responsibilities
- Train YOLO and Vision Transformers using PyTorch.
- Quantize models to INT8 using TensorRT and ONNX Runtime.
- Deploy inference models to NVIDIA Jetson devices.
- Benchmark FPS and latency bottlenecks.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years computer vision experience.
- PyTorch, OpenCV, TensorRT, ONNX.
- Edge deployment on ARM/Jetson.
- Deep knowledge of CNNs and Vision Transformers.
