# Information Security & Compliance Analyst
**Company**: TrustShield Governance
**Department**: Security Assurance
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Manage continuous security compliance frameworks, vendor risk assessments, and audit readiness.

## Key Responsibilities
- Review third-party vendor security postures and analyze SOC 2 reports and SIG questionnaires.
- Coordinate annual penetration testing and track remediation deadlines with engineering managers.
- Update internal security policies and conduct quarterly employee security awareness campaigns.
- Maintain the corporate Risk Register and document security exception requests.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in information security, IT audit, or compliance.
- Familiarity with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIST frameworks.
- Strong analytical, documentation, and stakeholder interview skills.
- Organized and structured communication.
