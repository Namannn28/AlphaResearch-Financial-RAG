# Senior Mobile Engineer (Flutter & Dart)
**Company**: CrossApp Studios
**Department**: Mobile Products
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Develop cross-platform iOS and Android consumer mobile applications using Flutter 3+ and Dart.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build reactive, clean state-managed user interfaces with Flutter Bloc or Riverpod.
- Integrate native platform channels for camera, Bluetooth, and biometric authentication.
- Optimize rendering frame rate (60/120 FPS) and eliminate UI jank.
- Maintain automated build and release distribution pipelines on Codemagic.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years dedicated mobile software development with 2+ years in production Flutter.
- Deep knowledge of Dart asynchronous programming (Streams, Futures).
- Experience publishing and maintaining apps on both App Store and Google Play.
- Strong UI/UX design sensibility.
