# InsurTech Claim Intelligence Engineer
**Company**: CoverSure Technologies
**Department**: Insurance Analytics
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Build predictive claims adjudication, fraud scoring, and computer vision damage assessment pipelines.

## Key Responsibilities
- Deploy computer vision models analyzing vehicle damage photos to automate repair cost estimation.
- Build automated fraud detection pipelines analyzing claim text, metadata, and historical records.
- Implement explainable AI models (SHAP values) to provide clear rationales for claim risk scores.
- Integrate with core insurance policy administration platforms.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in machine learning or data science within insurance or finance.
- Experience with computer vision (image classification, segmentation) and tabular ML (LightGBM).
- Focus on model interpretability (SHAP, LIME) and regulatory fairness.
- Python, SQL, and cloud deployment.
