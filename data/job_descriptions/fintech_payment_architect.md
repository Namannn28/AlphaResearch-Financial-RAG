# FinTech Payment Gateway Architect
**Company**: PayStream Global
**Department**: Core Payments
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Architect PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant card processing, tokenization, and multi-acquirer routing systems.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design payment processing services with idempotency keys and sub-100ms authorization latency.
- Implement card tokenization and secure credit card vaulting adhering to PCI-DSS standards.
- Optimize smart transaction routing across global acquiring banks to minimize processing fees.
- Manage payment webhook delivery and automated chargeback reconciliation.

## Required Qualifications
- 6+ years building FinTech or payment processing infrastructure.
- Deep understanding of card network rails (Visa, Mastercard), ISO 8583, and tokenization.
- Strong distributed systems design in Java, Go, or Python.
- Proven record of PCI-DSS audit compliance.
