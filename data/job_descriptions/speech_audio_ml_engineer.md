# Speech & Audio ML Engineer (ASR / TTS)
**Company**: SoundFlow Tech
**Department**: Audio AI
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Train and deploy low-latency automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS) neural models.

## Key Responsibilities
- Fine-tune Whisper and Conformer architectures for domain-specific vocabulary.
- Optimize neural vocoder inference (HiFi-GAN) for real-time conversational streaming.
- Build acoustic noise reduction and voice activity detection (VAD) algorithms.
- Deploy audio models using C++ and ONNX Runtime.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in speech processing or audio machine learning.
- Deep knowledge of acoustic feature extraction (MFCCs, spectrograms).
- PyTorch, HuggingFace, Kaldi or NeMo.
- Experience with streaming audio protocols (WebRTC).
