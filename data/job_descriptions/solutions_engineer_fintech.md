# Senior FinTech Solutions Engineer (Pre-Sales & Integration)
**Company**: PaySphere Tech
**Department**: Commercial Solutions
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Bridge enterprise sales and engineering by demonstrating payment APIs and guiding customer integrations.

## Key Responsibilities
- Deliver high-impact technical demonstrations and architectural walkthroughs for enterprise prospects.
- Guide customer engineering teams through API integration, webhooks, and sandbox testing.
- Author technical RFP/RFI responses and document integration edge cases.
- Provide direct customer feedback to product managers to prioritize feature roadmaps.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years in solutions engineering, sales engineering, or technical consulting in FinTech.
- Hands-on experience with REST APIs, authentication protocols (OAuth, HMAC), and JSON schemas.
- Strong coding skills in Python, Node.js, or Java for integration proofs-of-concept.
- Persuasive presentation and client leadership skills.
