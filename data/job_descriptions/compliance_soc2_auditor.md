# Technical Security Compliance Specialist (SOC 2 / ISO 27001)
**Company**: AuditReady Tech
**Department**: Security Compliance
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Lead technical evidence collection, continuous compliance automation, and external security audits.

## Key Responsibilities
- Automate evidence collection using compliance platforms (Vanta, Drata) integrated with AWS and GitHub.
- Coordinate annual SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA third-party independent audits.
- Conduct internal security risk assessments and maintain the corporate Risk Register.
- Train new employees on security awareness and acceptable use policies.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in security compliance or IT audit.
- CISA, CISSP, or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certification preferred.
- Experience with automated compliance software (Vanta, Drata).
- Thorough, organized, and detail-oriented work ethic.
