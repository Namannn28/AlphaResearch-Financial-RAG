# Master Data Management (MDM) Architect
**Company**: GlobalCorp Enterprise
**Department**: Enterprise Data
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Design customer and product master data harmonization, deduplication, and entity resolution systems.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build deterministic and probabilistic entity resolution pipelines using machine learning.
- Establish golden record creation workflows across disparate CRM and ERP records.
- Implement real-time master data synchronization via REST APIs and Kafka.
- Conduct steering committee reviews with enterprise business units.

## Required Qualifications
- 6+ years in master data management (MDM) or enterprise data integration.
- Experience with entity resolution algorithms and fuzzy matching (Levenshtein, Jaro-Winkler).
- Strong database and data modeling expertise.
- Enterprise stakeholder management skills.
