# Senior BI & Dashboard Engineer (Tableau / Power BI)
**Company**: InsightHub Analytics
**Department**: Business Intelligence
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Design intuitive, responsive analytical dashboards delivering operational insights to company leadership.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build interactive, executive-ready dashboards in Tableau and Microsoft Power BI.
- Optimize underlying SQL analytical views and data extraction schedules to ensure fast load times.
- Implement row-level security (RLS) to ensure data confidentiality across departments.
- Conduct user research with business analysts to refine visual UX and dashboard ergonomics.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years building enterprise dashboards in Tableau or Power BI.
- Advanced SQL writing complex CTEs, window functions, and aggregations.
- Understanding of visual perception, color theory, and chart choice fundamentals.
- Strong stakeholder management skills.
