# Senior Enterprise Solutions Architect
**Company**: ScaleBridge Solutions
**Department**: Solutions Engineering
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Partner with enterprise sales teams to architect technical solutions and guide customer proof-of-concepts (POCs).

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead technical discovery calls and map customer architectural requirements to product capabilities.
- Build and present custom technical Proof-of-Concept (POC) demonstrations for enterprise clients.
- Address technical security, compliance, and integration objections from customer architects.
- Author whitepapers and reference architectures for common integration patterns.

## Required Qualifications
- 5+ years in pre-sales solutions engineering or enterprise architecture.
- Broad understanding of cloud infrastructure, APIs, and security protocols.
- Exceptional presentation and demonstration skills.
- Ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical executives.
