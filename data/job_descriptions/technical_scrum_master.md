# Agile Coach & Technical Scrum Master
**Company**: AgileFlow Consulting
**Department**: Agile Office
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Facilitate agile ceremonies, remove engineering blockers, and optimize team delivery velocity across 4 squads.

## Key Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, backlog refinement, and insightful sprint retrospectives.
- Identify and eliminate cross-team dependencies and engineering blockers.
- Track team metrics: sprint velocity, burndown, cycle time, and pull request review turnaround.
- Foster a blameless culture of continuous engineering improvement and psychological safety.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years as a Scrum Master or Agile Coach in technical software engineering environments.
- Certified Scrum Master (CSM) or PMI-ACP certification.
- Deep knowledge of Jira workflows, automation, and agile reporting.
- High emotional intelligence and servant leadership mindset.
