# Reinforcement Learning Engineer
**Company**: OptimalPath AI
**Department**: Decision Intelligence
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Design reinforcement learning algorithms for robotics manipulation and dynamic resource allocation.

## Key Responsibilities
- Implement deep RL algorithms (PPO, SAC, TD3) in simulation environments (Isaac Gym, MuJoCo).
- Design reward shaping functions and domain randomization techniques for sim-to-real transfer.
- Optimize policy network inference on onboard embedded hardware.
- Author technical documentation and benchmark results.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years experience with reinforcement learning and optimal control.
- PyTorch, Stable-Baselines3, or RLlib.
- Experience with physics simulators (MuJoCo, Isaac Gym).
- Strong mathematical foundation in MDPs and stochastic calculus.
