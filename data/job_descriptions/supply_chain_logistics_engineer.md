# Supply Chain Systems & Routing Engineer
**Company**: LogiTrack Solutions
**Department**: Logistics Tech
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Develop vehicle routing optimization and real-time inventory tracking engines for global freight networks.

## Key Responsibilities
- Implement vehicle routing problem (VRP) optimization algorithms using Google OR-Tools and Python.
- Track GPS fleet movements and calculate dynamic Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) updates.
- Integrate with third-party logistics APIs, customs databases, and barcode warehouse scanners.
- Build real-time tracking dashboards for logistics dispatchers.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years in logistics software or operational research.
- Experience with combinatorial optimization (OR-Tools, genetic algorithms).
- Strong Python and spatial database skills (PostGIS).
- Familiarity with supply chain EDI protocols.
