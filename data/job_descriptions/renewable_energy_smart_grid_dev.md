# Smart Grid & Energy Analytics Engineer
**Company**: VoltGrid Systems
**Department**: CleanTech Intelligence
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Develop predictive time-series models for solar/wind energy generation forecasting and battery dispatch optimization.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build time-series forecasting models (Temporal Fusion Transformers, Prophet) for renewable power output.
- Optimize battery energy storage system (BESS) charge/discharge schedules against dynamic electricity pricing.
- Ingest supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) telemetry over DNP3 and Modbus protocols.
- Deliver automated grid stability reporting to utility operators.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in energy analytics, smart grid tech, or industrial IoT.
- Strong time-series forecasting experience in Python.
- Understanding of electrical grid fundamentals and wholesale electricity markets.
- Experience with industrial communication protocols.
