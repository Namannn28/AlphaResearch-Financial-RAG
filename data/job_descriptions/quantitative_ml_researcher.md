# Quantitative ML Researcher
**Company**: AlphaMetrics Capital
**Department**: Algorithmic Trading
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Develop predictive statistical and machine learning alpha signals for high-frequency financial markets.

## Key Responsibilities
- Research non-linear predictive signals using gradient boosting and temporal graph neural networks.
- Clean and normalize tick-level limit order book (LOB) financial data.
- Backtest trading strategies accounting for market impact, slippage, and execution latency.
- Collaborate with quant developers to deploy models into ultra-low-latency execution engines.

## Required Qualifications
- MS or PhD in Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, or Computer Science.
- Proficiency in Python (NumPy, Pandas, Scipy, Polars) and C++.
- Deep understanding of time-series analysis and financial market microstructure.
- Experience working with limit order book data.
