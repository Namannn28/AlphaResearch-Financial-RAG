# LegalTech Document Intelligence Engineer
**Company**: LexiAI Solutions
**Department**: Legal Intelligence
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Build specialized document parsing, contract clause extraction, and semantic search systems for law firms.

## Key Responsibilities
- Train and fine-tune OCR and layout analysis models on dense legal contracts and court filings.
- Implement specialized RAG pipelines cross-referencing statutory codes and legal precedents.
- Extract key contract obligations, indemnification clauses, and expiration dates with high precision.
- Ensure strict legal privilege data segregation and client matter confidentiality.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in NLP, document understanding, or LegalTech applications.
- Experience with document parsing (LayoutLM, PDFMiner, PyMuPDF) and RAG.
- Understanding of legal document structures and vocabulary.
- High bar for factual accuracy and zero hallucination.
