# Observability & Telemetry Infrastructure Engineer
**Company**: TracePoint Systems
**Department**: Observability Core
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Build unified distributed tracing, metrics, and log aggregation platforms for high-scale microservices.

## Key Responsibilities
- Deploy and manage OpenTelemetry collectors, Grafana Tempo, Prometheus, and VictoriaMetrics.
- Implement standardized distributed trace propagation headers (W3C Trace Context) across services.
- Optimize telemetry ingestion pipelines and author metric drop-rules to curb Datadog billings.
- Create operational dashboards and high-signal alert rules.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years in observability or SRE roles.
- Experience with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK Stack.
- Deep understanding of metrics (RED/USE methods), traces, and structured logging.
- Python or Go scripting skills.
