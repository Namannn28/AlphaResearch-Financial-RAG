# Developer Advocate & Technical Evangelist
**Company**: OpenAI Tooling
**Department**: Developer Relations
**Location**: Multiple Locations / Remote

## Role Overview
Inspire and educate global software developers through open-source sample apps, technical blogs, and conferences.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build open-source demonstration applications showcasing modern RAG and generative AI patterns.
- Author deep-dive technical tutorials, blog posts, and comprehensive documentation.
- Speak at major technical conferences (PyCon, KubeCon, AWS re:Invent) and host developer workshops.
- Gather community feedback and advocate internally for developer product improvements.

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years as a software engineer or developer advocate.
- Strong public speaking and engaging technical writing skills.
- Active presence on GitHub, YouTube, or developer communities.
- Passion for teaching and community building.
