# Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) - Form 10-K Annual Report (Fiscal Year 2023)

## Document Metadata
- Company: Tesla, Inc.
- Ticker: TSLA
- Fiscal Year: 2023 (Ended December 31, 2023)
- Industry: Automotive, Energy Storage & Autonomous Driving AI

---

## Item 1. Business Overview
Tesla designs, develops, manufactures, sells, and leases high-performance fully electric vehicles, energy storage systems (Powerwall, Megapack), and solar energy generation products. Tesla is also developing artificial intelligence software including Full Self-Driving (FSD) Supervised, autonomous robotaxis, and the Optimus humanoid robot.

---

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

### Consolidated Financial Performance
- **Total Revenues**: $96,773 million ($96.77 billion) in fiscal 2023, compared to $81,462 million in fiscal 2022, representing an increase of 18.8% year-over-year.
- **Cost of Revenues**: $79,113 million ($79.11 billion).
- **Total Gross Profit**: $17,660 million ($17.66 billion), resulting in a gross margin of 18.2%, down sharply from 25.6% in 2022 due to aggressive global vehicle price reductions.
- **Operating Income**: $8,891 million ($8.89 billion), down 35% compared to $13,656 million in 2022.
- **Net Income**: $14,997 million ($15.00 billion), which included a non-cash tax benefit of $5.9 billion from the release of valuation allowances on deferred tax assets.
- **Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $4.30.

### Segment Revenue Breakdown (FY 2023)
- **Automotive Revenues**: $82,419 million ($82.42 billion), an increase of 15% year-over-year. Vehicle deliveries reached 1,808,581 units (primarily Model 3 and Model Y), up 38% year-over-year. Automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits declined to 17.1%.
- **Energy Generation and Storage**: $6,035 million ($6.04 billion), surging 54% year-over-year, driven by Megapack deployment volumes of 14.7 GWh.
- **Services and Other**: $8,319 million ($8.32 billion), including Supercharging network fees, paid maintenance, insurance, and merchandise.

### R&D and Capital Investments
- **Research and Development (R&D)**: $3,969 million ($3.97 billion) in FY 2023, representing 4.1% of revenue. Major engineering focus areas included the Cybertruck production ramp, 4680 battery cell manufacturing, custom Dojo AI supercomputing chips, and end-to-end neural network autonomous driving models (FSD V12).
- **Capital Expenditures (Capex)**: $8,899 million ($8.90 billion), funding factory tooling in Texas and Berlin, Shanghai expansion, and AI data compute clusters.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
1. **Automotive Pricing Pressure and Margin Compression**: Industry-wide electric vehicle price competition, rising interest rates on auto financing, and consumer trade-downs forced multiple price reductions, compressing vehicle gross margins from 28.5% in 2021 to 18.2% in 2023.
2. **Autonomous Driving Regulatory and Safety Scrutiny**: Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) software face active investigations by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the California DMV regarding marketing disclosures and driver monitoring.
3. **Key Person Dependency**: Tesla's operational leadership and public visibility remain highly dependent on the continued active management of CEO Elon Musk.
