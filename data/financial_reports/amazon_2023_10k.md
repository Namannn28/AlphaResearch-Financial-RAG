# Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) - Form 10-K Annual Report (Fiscal Year 2023)

## Document Metadata
- Company: Amazon.com, Inc.
- Ticker: AMZN
- Fiscal Year: 2023 (Ended December 31, 2023)
- Industry: E-Commerce, Cloud Infrastructure (AWS) & Digital Advertising

---

## Item 1. Business Overview
Amazon serves consumers through online and physical stores, manufacturers and content creators through publishing and seller services, and enterprises through Amazon Web Services (AWS). Amazon's reportable segments are North America, International, and AWS.

---

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

### Consolidated Financial Performance
- **Total Net Sales**: $574,785 million ($574.78 billion) in fiscal 2023, compared to $513,983 million in 2022, an increase of 11.8% year-over-year.
- **Cost of Sales**: $304,527 million ($304.53 billion).
- **Gross Profit**: $270,258 million ($270.26 billion), with a gross margin of 47.0%, compared to 43.8% in 2022, supported by regionalized logistics fulfillment networks.
- **Operating Income**: $36,852 million ($36.85 billion), surging from $12,248 million in 2022 as cost-efficiency measures took effect.
- **Net Income**: $30,425 million ($30.43 billion), rebounding strongly from a net loss of $2,722 million in 2022 (which had included a valuation loss on Rivian stock).
- **Diluted EPS**: $2.90.

### Segment Revenue Breakdown (FY 2023)
- **North America**: $352,828 million ($352.83 billion), up 12% year-over-year. Segment operating income improved to $14,877 million compared to an operating loss in 2022.
- **AWS (Amazon Web Services)**: $90,757 million ($90.76 billion), up 13.3% year-over-year, accounting for 15.8% of total revenue. AWS generated $24,631 million ($24.63 billion) in operating income, representing 66.8% of Amazon's total consolidated operating profit.
- **International**: $131,200 million ($131.20 billion), up 11% year-over-year, with an operating loss of $2,656 million.
- **Advertising Services**: $46,906 million ($46.91 billion), growing 24.3% year-over-year, driven by sponsored products and video ads.

### R&D (Technology & Content) and Capex
- **Technology and Content (R&D equivalent)**: $85,622 million ($85.62 billion) in FY 2023, including investments in AWS generative AI infrastructure (Amazon Bedrock, Trainium and Inferentia chips), warehouse robotics, and streaming content.
- **Capital Expenditures (Capex)**: $48,100 million ($48.10 billion), reallocated from fulfillment center footprints into AWS cloud and AI computing capacity.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
1. **Cloud Cost Optimization and AI Competition**: AWS revenue growth moderated to 13.3% as enterprise customers optimized existing cloud spend. Intense competition from Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud on generative AI workloads requires sustained capital intensity.
2. **Global Logistics and Regional Fulfillment Volatility**: Operating physical fulfillment and delivery networks exposes Amazon to energy prices, wage inflation, and supply chain bottlenecks across global fulfillment centers.
3. **Regulatory Scrutiny on E-Commerce Marketplace Practices**: The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and European regulatory bodies maintain ongoing antitrust challenges regarding marketplace fees, third-party merchant data usage, and Prime bundling.
