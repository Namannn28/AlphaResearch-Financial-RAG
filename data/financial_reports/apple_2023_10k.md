# Apple Inc. (AAPL) - Form 10-K Annual Report (Fiscal Year 2023)

## Document Metadata
- Company: Apple Inc.
- Ticker: AAPL
- Fiscal Year: 2023 (Ended September 30, 2023)
- Industry: Consumer Electronics, Software & Cloud Services

---

## Item 1. Business Overview
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The company's key product categories include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home and Accessories. Key services include Advertising, AppleCare, Cloud Services, Digital Content (App Store, Apple Music, Apple TV+), and Payment Services (Apple Pay, Apple Card).

---

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

### Consolidated Financial Performance
- **Total Net Sales**: $383,285 million ($383.29 billion) in fiscal 2023, compared to $394,328 million in fiscal 2022, representing a decrease of 2.8% due to macroeconomic headwinds and foreign exchange volatility.
- **Total Cost of Sales**: $214,137 million ($214.14 billion).
- **Gross Margin**: $169,148 million ($169.15 billion), with a gross margin percentage of 44.1%, up from 43.3% in fiscal 2022, driven by services mix and favorable component costs.
- **Operating Income**: $114,301 million ($114.30 billion), compared to $119,437 million in fiscal 2022.
- **Net Income**: $96,995 million ($97.00 billion), generating diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $6.13.

### Revenue Breakdown by Product & Service Segment (FY 2023)
- **iPhone**: $200,583 million ($200.58 billion), accounting for 52.3% of total revenue.
- **Services**: $85,200 million ($85.20 billion), representing an all-time revenue record and growing 9.1% year-over-year.
- **Wearables, Home & Accessories**: $39,845 million ($39.85 billion), including Apple Watch, AirPods, and HomePod.
- **Mac**: $29,357 million ($29.36 billion), down 27% year-over-year due to difficult comparisons with the M2 release in prior periods.
- **iPad**: $28,300 million ($28.30 billion), down 3.4% year-over-year.

### Operating Expenses & Research and Development (R&D)
- **Research and Development (R&D)**: $29,915 million ($29.92 billion) in FY 2023, compared to $26,251 million in FY 2022. R&D represented 7.8% of net sales, focused on custom silicon (Apple M-series and A-series), artificial intelligence and machine learning architectures, and mixed-reality spatial computing (Apple Vision Pro).
- **Selling, General, and Administrative (SG&A)**: $24,932 million ($24.93 billion).
- **Capital Expenditures (Capex)**: $10,959 million ($10.96 billion) dedicated to manufacturing tooling, data center expansion, and facilities.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
1. **Supply Chain Concentration**: Substantially all manufacturing is performed by outsourced partners in Asia, primarily China and Taiwan. Geopolitical tensions or regional disruptions could materially impact device delivery.
2. **App Store Regulatory and Legal Pressure**: Global antitrust investigations (including the European Union Digital Markets Act and US Department of Justice) may require changes to the 30% App Store commission and allow alternative payment systems, potentially reducing Services gross margins.
3. **Foreign Exchange Risk**: Over 55% of Apple's net sales are generated outside the United States, exposing results to foreign currency fluctuations against the US Dollar.
